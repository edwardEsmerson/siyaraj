Contact: front leg extended forward with heel touching the ground, back leg straight behind on its toes, opposite arm swung forward. Empty fists, no weapon; Khara's gada is a separate component
Down: front foot flat and knee bent taking the weight, back foot lifting off, body at its lowest. Empty fists, no weapon; Khara's gada is a separate component
Passing: weight on the straight front leg, back leg passing under the body with knee lifted, arms near the sides. Empty fists, no weapon; Khara's gada is a separate component
Up: pushing off the toes of the standing leg, body at its highest, free leg swinging forward. Empty fists, no weapon; Khara's gada is a separate component
