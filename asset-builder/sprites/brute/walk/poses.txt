Contact: front leg extended forward with heel touching the ground, back leg straight behind on its toes, opposite arm swung forward. A heavy stomp
Down: front foot flat and knee bent taking the weight, back foot lifting off, body at its lowest. A heavy stomp
Passing: weight on the straight front leg, back leg passing under the body with knee lifted, arms near the sides. A heavy stomp
Up: pushing off the toes of the standing leg, body at its highest, free leg swinging forward. A heavy stomp
